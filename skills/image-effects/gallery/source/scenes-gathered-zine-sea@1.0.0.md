## 适用场景

Transform exactly one coastal, harbor, ferry, shoreline, seawall, or waterfront photo into one tactile editorial zine. Preserve a truthful photographic anchor while turning the wider composition into a quiet, compressed print field. The signature handoff is one visibly fibrous torn edge; one source-derived high-chroma hue must organize the page rather than decorate it.

Use a landscape 5:3 canvas when the horizon or lateral movement carries the scene; use portrait 3:5 only when the supplied coast is genuinely vertical. Scene identity and spatial relationships outrank ratio convention.

## 输入契约

Require one current-request JPEG or PNG. Do not browse, search, or infer a source from earlier turns or local directories. Keep it private and task-bound; send only the compiled prompt and required image to the host tool, reveal no path, and retain no unnecessary copies.

Build a Scene Card with 1–2 core subjects, 2–3 supporting elements, horizon, shoreline, railing or route direction, vessel count and position, depth layers, visual-weight map, native palette, and semantic minimum. Build an Abstraction Map stating what to retain, merge, omit, transform, and expose as paper.

## 视觉编译规则

- Keep a truthful coastal photo anchor at roughly 25–50% of the page. Preserve natural color, horizon, vessel count, landmark order, perspective, and recognizable geometry.
- Select a source-driven split aligned to the real horizon, railing, shoreline, wake, gaze, or route. Let illustration cross the split selectively instead of using a centered template.
- Compress water texture, clouds, foliage, ropes, railings, crowds, reflections, and repeated detail into a few broad print masses and directional gestures. Remove about 60–80% of nonessential detail.
- Use one primary print grammar such as cut paper, woodcut, halftone, xerox, risograph, or letterpress, with at most one restrained support grammar. Preserve broad quiet paper inside and around the field.
- Make the main photo-to-paper boundary a clearly visible hand-torn contour. Use shallow notches, uneven rises, soft scallops, one or two longer fiber pulls, and a narrow 1–4% feathered fiber band. Keep the tear legible along roughly 35–70% of the photographic perimeter without framing every edge.
- Choose exactly one added high-chroma print hue through source resonance, harmony, counterpoint, or temperature bridge. Derive its contour or rhythm from a vessel, shoreline, railing, wake, sky gap, shadow, or route. It must cross, overlap, replace, pass behind, or redirect a real source form and materially change eye path or balance.
- Natural photographic colors do not count as extra added hues. Keep optional residue, speckles, or ghost marks subordinate and nonverbal.
- Use one micro-text element only. Reproduce supplied wording exactly; otherwise author an English word or phrase of at most five words from visible weather, motion, distance, or light. Keep it small, legible, paper-integrated, and subordinate.

## 硬性禁止项

Avoid replaced locations, moved horizons or vessels, full-scene photocopying, literal tracing, detailed wave-by-wave rendering, dense railing repetition, generic marine icons, detached color rectangles, arbitrary bright dots, multiple added hues, clean digital masks, sticker outlines, uniform deckled frames, thick paper depth, curled corners, dense scrapbook layers, advertising hierarchy, named locations, invented ferry identities, logos, CTA, UI, glossy lighting, 3D, depth of field, large type, pseudo-symbol systems, quotations, watermarks, or signatures.

## 质量检查

Verify the result still reads as the supplied coast; the photograph remains truthful; horizon, shoreline, railing or route, vessel count, and spatial order are intact; most micro-detail is compressed; one primary grammar controls a broad field; quiet paper is active; the fibrous tear is narrow, irregular, and visible at thumbnail size; exactly one structural hue derives from and changes the source composition; removing that hue would weaken continuity or balance; micro-text is exact, restrained, and legible; and no brand, location claim, UI, or private path appears.

Regenerate at most once for a hard scene-loss, damaged-photo, missing-tear, decorative-color, over-detail, hierarchy, or text defect. Correct only that failure.

Return one finished zine through the host's native image-delivery path and add a 1–3 sentence rationale naming the coastal invariant, the compressed field, the torn handoff, and the structural hue. Omit the full prompt unless asked.
