# Boundaries for private video study notes

## Default safe mode

Use transcript and timestamp links first. Do not download full videos by default.

## Authorized frame mode

Frame extraction is appropriate when:

- the video is user-owned;
- the user has permission;
- the video is a local file supplied by the user;
- the user explicitly requests local private-study extraction and accepts the risk.

Operational limits:

- extract only short sections around selected timestamps;
- keep low or medium resolution frames unless high resolution is needed for OCR/code;
- store provenance: source URL/file, timestamp, extraction date, and reason;
- do not redistribute screenshots.

## Image generation boundary

Generated images should be original explanatory visuals:

- knowledge maps;
- flowcharts;
- hand-drawn review cards;
- concept diagrams.

Do not ask the image model to recreate the video's copyrighted visual style, creator likeness, or screenshots.
